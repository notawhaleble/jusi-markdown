# Mathematical reading

Inline energy $E = mc^2$ and a probability \(P(A \mid B) = \frac{P(B \mid A)P(A)}{P(B)}\).

## Integrals and derivations

$$
\begin{aligned}
\int_0^1 x^2\,dx &= \left[\frac{x^3}{3}\right]_0^1 \\
                 &= \frac{1}{3}.
\end{aligned}
$$
